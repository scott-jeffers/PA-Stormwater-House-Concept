What goes in the erosion & sediment plan
  • Limit of disturbance: outline the house, deck, garage, septic bed, utility trench and stockpile area. Keep it compact.
  • Silt sock (12–18 in. compost filter sock) along the downhill edge, following the contour
  • Stone construction entrance (AASHTO #1 stone) where trucks leave the work area. Sweep up any mud on the road right away
  • Topsoil stockpile uphill, ringed with sock, and seeded if it will sit more than a few weeks
  • Sequence: sock and entrance → clear and strip → foundation → septic and utilities → final grade → topsoil, seed and mulch → remove the sock at 70% grass cover
  • Inspect after every storm and fix anything damaged. Keep a simple log

Why care: the lot drains to a tributary of Tohickon Creek that DEP lists as impaired by siltation. Good erosion control here genuinely matters.

Drainage rules that still apply (even when exempt)
  • Roof drains must discharge to infiltration areas or vegetated areas (lawn, rain garden), never to the street or the sanitary system (§802.A–B)
  • Sump pumps and basement drains on lots over 20,000 sf must discharge to a natural watercourse, drainage swale or storm sewer (§802.C)
  • The exemption doesn't excuse measures needed to protect health, safety and property (§106.C). Don't concentrate water onto neighbors or the road
  • Keep downspouts and any infiltration at least 25 ft from the septic beds and the well. Grade 6 in. of fall in the first 10 ft away from the foundation

Not exempt? Cheap fixes, plus a sizing calculator

If the driveway is new, the total is 8,194 sf, which is 694 sf over the limit. In order of cost:

  • Slatted deck over a stone bed. Decks built to let water soak in aren't counted (§202). That brings the total to 7,182 sf, under the limit
  • Narrow the driveway. Going from about 14.6 ft to 12 ft saves about 850 sf
  • Small infiltration plan. Capture the first 2 in. of runoff from new roofs and paving in dry wells, trenches or a rain garden (§303 simplified method). A one-sheet design with a PE stamp

What was checked, and the answers
  • Which rules apply: Bedminster Township Stormwater Ordinance No. 239, adopted Oct 14, 2020 and effective Oct 19, 2020. Impervious surface counts only if built on or after that date
  • Size test (Table 106.1): lot over 5 acres → 7,500 sf allowed. Proposed: 3,381 sf ✓
  • Location test (Table 106.2): no more than 5,000 sf within 200 ft of the downslope line, 4,000 within 100 ft, and none within 10 ft. Driveway access is excluded. All 3,381 sf sits 100–200 ft from the line ✓
  • Stream status: the lot drains to a tributary of Tohickon Creek, which Chapter 93 lists as Cold Water Fishes (CWF). It is not High Quality or Exceptional Value, so no extra volume rules apply ✓
  • State permits: a written E&S plan is required at 5,000 sf or more of disturbance. An NPDES permit is required only at 1 acre or more ✓
  • E&S approval: the township must approve E&S plans only for non-exempt projects (§310.B). Exempt projects still keep the state-required plan on site (§310.C, E)
  • Fees: stormwater application $100, exemption $500 and escrow $750 (2022 schedule) ✓
  • Still to confirm: the driveway date, the township's reading of the gravel drive and deck, whether they want county E&S review, and current fees
