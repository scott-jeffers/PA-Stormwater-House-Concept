Best practices for this home

Worth doing whether or not a permit requires it. Each card is tagged: State law Township rule Recommended

State law · during construction

Silt sock or fence below all bare soil

  • Before digging, run 12–18 in. compost filter sock (or silt fence) along the downhill edge of the work, on the contour
  • Turn the ends uphill so water can't run around them
  • Ring the topsoil pile and keep it uphill
  • Check after every rain. Clean it out when it's half full
Photo: U.S. EPA, public domain
Recommended · and it helps the exemption

Keep the driveway narrow

  • 12 ft is plenty for one house. Every foot narrower saves about 330 sf on this 330-ft drive
  • A two-track "ribbon" drive with grass in the middle is the lowest-impact option
  • Add broad dips or stone water bars every 75–100 ft to turn runoff onto grass, not into the road
  • Keep the culvert at the road clear
Photo: Casimir Kromkowski, CC0
Township rule · Ord. 239 §802

Downspouts to lawn, not to pipes or the road

  • Roof leaders must discharge to lawn or vegetated areas, or to infiltration
  • Extend each one at least 10 ft from the foundation onto a splash pad or into a rain garden
  • Never into the septic, never piped to the road
  • Sump and footing drains go to a swale or natural drainage path (lots over 20,000 sf)
Photo: AGreenFutureCalgaryLandscaping, CC BY-SA 4.0
Recommended

A rain garden downhill of the house

  • A shallow (6 in.) planted dip that catches roof water and lets it soak in
  • Rough size for the 1,400 sf house roof: about 230 sf (1 in. of rain)
  • Keep it 10–20 ft from the foundation and 25 ft or more from the septic beds and well
  • Use native plants, and amend clay soil with compost
Photo: U.S. EPA, public domain
Recommended

Plant, don't pave

  • Leave as much of the lot in meadow or woods as possible. It soaks up rain for free
  • A strip of unmown meadow along the road edge slows runoff and catches sediment
  • Seed and mulch bare soil within days, not weeks
Photo: U.S. EPA, public domain
Recommended · cheap

Rain barrels for garden water

  • A 50–60 gal barrel on a garage downspout waters the garden
  • Fit a screened lid and route the overflow to lawn or the rain garden
  • Empty it before winter
Photo: U.S. EPA, public domain
Optional · only if needed

Stone dry well for a downspout

  • A gravel-filled pit (about 3 ft deep) that soaks up roof water where a rain garden won't fit
  • At least 20 ft from the foundation. Build it after the site is stabilized
  • Needs 18 in. or more above rock or seasonal high water
Photo: SuSanA Secretariat, CC BY 2.0
Optional · if the exemption fails

Stone trench along the driveway

  • A shallow stone-filled trench along the downhill edge of the drive catches and soaks in its runoff
  • This is the kind of BMP a small engineered plan would use if needed
Photo: Moreau1, public domain

More construction must-dos (state law and township rules)

  • Call 811 (PA One Call) at least 3 business days before digging
  • Stone construction entrance (AASHTO #1 stone) where trucks leave the site. Sweep any mud off the road the same day (§310.F)
  • Fence off the septic beds with orange fence. No traffic or stockpiles on them, ever
  • Grade 6 in. of fall in the first 10 ft away from the foundation
  • Seed and mulch any area left idle more than 4 days. Keep stockpiles under 35 ft high, with 2:1 sides
  • Keep the E&S plan on site, and keep total disturbance under 1 acre

Details

Rules checked, and the answers
  • Ordinance: Bedminster Stormwater Ordinance No. 239 (adopted Oct 14, 2020; effective Oct 19, 2020). Only impervious surface built on or after that date counts
  • Table 106.1 (size): lot over 5 acres → 7,500 sf. As drawn 8,503 ✗. With the fix 6,615 ✓
  • Table 106.2 (location): ≤5,000 sf within 200 ft, ≤4,000 within 100 ft, none within 10 ft of the downslope line, with driveway access excluded. The buildings sit 100–200 ft from the line ✓
  • Impervious definition (§202): decks, parking areas and driveways aren't counted if designed and built as a BMP that promotes infiltration. Gravel isn't addressed separately, so the township decides
  • Pervious-paving credit (Appendix J): only for slopes of 1–5%. This driveway is about 8%, so it doesn't qualify
  • Stream: Deer Run → Tohickon Creek (CWF, MF) in Chapter 93. Not HQ/EV ✓
  • E&S: written plan at 5,000 sf or more of disturbance; NPDES at 1 acre or more. Township E&S approval is required only for non-exempt projects (§310.B)
  • Roof and sump drains (§802): apply to everyone, exempt or not
  • Zoning: AP district. 8,503 sf is about 2.3% of the lot, far below the impervious ratios listed in the zoning ordinance (6–10% for most uses), but confirm the AP single-family limit
